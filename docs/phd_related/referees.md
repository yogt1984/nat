# Referees — the campaign's real blocker

**Status: 0 of 3 confirmed.** EDFI wants 2–3 letters. This is the single item most likely to sink a
January application, and it is the one that cannot be produced in December — letters take weeks, and
a referee who has not heard from you in years needs lead time and a reason to say yes.

Fill this in by **end of September**. If slot 3 is still empty in November, that is the signal that
the outreach strategy is not working and needs changing, not resending.

**The ask.** Do not open with "will you write me a letter". Open with an update — what you have been
building, that you are applying, and a link to the SSRN page. Attach the CV and the one-page summary
of the eight preprints so they have something concrete to write *from*. A referee who has lost track
of you writes a weak letter unless you give them material. Then ask.

**If they are unreachable or would decline:** any other academic who supervised or examined
substantial quantitative work. A weak academic letter still outranks no academic letter, because its
absence is what the committee notices.

**Choose for content, not seniority.** The useful letter says "this person designed and shipped
systems whose correctness mattered, and exercised independent technical judgement" — not "punctual
and hard-working". An engineering manager who watched you build something hard is worth more than a
distant executive.

This slot is the entire reason the outreach ask is laddered: arXiv endorsement → call → pre-doc/RA →
supervision. A professor who has endorsed a preprint, taken a call, and seen the codebase can write a
real letter. One who received a cold email cannot.

**Timeline reality:** to have a letter by Jan 15, the relationship must exist by late November. That
means Tier 1 goes out in **mid-October at the latest**.

## What to send every referee, once, in one message

- CV (`cv.tex` → PDF)
- `docs/research/onepagers/all_preprints_1p.pdf` — the whole body of work on one sheet
- The SSRN author page link
- The research proposal (`research_proposal.tex` → PDF)
- The deadline, stated plainly, with three weeks of margin

## Do not

- Ask in December. Letters written under time pressure read that way.
- Send eight PDFs. Send the one-pager and let them ask.
- Overstate. If a referee repeats a claim you have since retired — the combiner, the convolver
  kernels as a result — it damages you and embarrasses them. The `[!]` blocks in
  `docs/research/onepagers/` are the list of what not to hand anyone.
