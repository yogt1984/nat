\cvsec{Independent research (2025--2026)}

Built and operate an end-to-end quantitative research platform for cryptocurrency perpetual futures,
and wrote up its results as eight working papers.

\entry{Instrumentation}{Rust ingestion engine subscribing to full order-book and trade streams;
  236-dimensional feature vector per symbol every 100\,ms across 21 categories; end-to-end feature
  latency below 80\,ms at the 99th percentile. Write-once capture store with dual event/ingest
  timestamps, making lookahead a queryable property rather than a review convention.}
\entry{Research layer}{Autonomous discovery agents under a five-gate replication protocol with
  Benjamini--Hochberg false-discovery-rate control and an adaptive acceptance threshold; hash-chained
  verdict ledger; walk-forward validation with purging and embargo. Python/Rust, roughly 4{,}300
  automated tests, a 340-command research CLI.}
\entry{Datasets held}{Tick-level order book, trade and funding data on BTC, ETH and SOL at 100\,ms
  cadence; position-level liquidation data from a venue publishing per-position liquidation prices.}
