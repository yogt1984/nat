\opsec{1 \ convolver — Event-Aligned SVD \hfill 15pp}
Analytically-defined events (breakout / turtle-soup / trap) $\times$ 4 channels (body, wicks, volume)
$\to$ SVD $\to$ IC gate with BH-FDR. \textbf{SVD never sees forward returns} — the structural
anti-overfit argument. 7.2\,M ticks, 12,059 candles, 1,775 events. \textbf{6 of 158 kernels survive FDR},
$|\IC|\in[0.20,0.36]$; \textbf{no breakout kernel survives}. Rolling stability $\bar\rho>0.90$ for the
strongest. \caution{No kernel passes OOS robustness — two sign-flip. Method is the result.}
\emph{P1 camera-ready target. Lead for Teichmann, Bölcskei, Malamud, Cheridito, Zdeborová, Krzakala,
Filipović.}

\opsec{2 \ microstructure\_alpha — FDR-Controlled Discovery \hfill 12pp}
236 features, 100\,ms, BTC/ETH/SOL, 1\,s--15\,min. Imbalance dominates, $\IC\approx0.45$ at 1--5\,s,
universal across symbols, 7/8 robustness (BTC). \textbf{Central negative result:} conditioning on
directionally-correct passive fills collapses $\IC$ $0.45\to0.03$ on all three symbols — a structural
\textbf{adverse-selection barrier}; execution, not signal quality, binds. Five-gate protocol
(discovery/cost/temporal/cross-symbol/dedup) + BH-FDR $q{=}0.05$ + adaptive threshold + decay monitoring.
\caution{Combiner $\IC$ .18/.25/.36 on a thin window — those algorithms were REFUTED by the Q4 kill gate
2026-07-30. The barrier result is unaffected.}
\emph{Lead for Collin-Dufresne, Hugonnier, Acciaio, Bühlmann, Farkas, Kuhn.}

\opsec{3 \ liquidity\_heatmap — Liquidation Geometry \hfill 11pp}
$K{=}200$ bins, $10$\,bps, $\pm10\%$; 8 scale-invariant features. Domino condition
$\delta_B-\delta_A<\lambda M_A/m_t$, depth as damper, $\sgn(\mathrm{ACP})$ predicts sign. \textbf{No
performance numbers anywhere — deliberate.} Aug-2026 revision (§7): sampling rate now \emph{measured}
(coverage .315/.380/.247 at $s{=}2$), and a \textbf{second bias found} — 2.9\% of liquidated wallets vs
21.9\% of liquidated notional; the OI rescaling cannot repair it (assumes uniform thinning; this is
biased in shape). \caution{Mapped frac $0.0\%$ is confounded by snapshot ordering, not a refutation.}

\opsec{4 \ prism — Perception Pressure \& Resonance \hfill 7pp}
$P(K,t)=A(K,t)\cdot V(K,t)$: salience $\ge0$ times credibility-weighted valence in $[-1,+1]$.
Three properties novel in combination — multiplicative coupling (signed, magnitude-meaningful),
\textbf{diversity as multiplier} (one outlet cannot inflate it), full decomposability. Trust
\emph{earned} by automated cross-corroboration, $0.1+0.4\cdot(\text{validated}/\text{total})$, never
hand-assigned. Five-agent pipeline. System at \code{~/Prism}. \caution{Methods/system paper; no empirics
logged.} \emph{Lead for Leippold — send with \#5.}

\opsec{5 \ prism\_signal — From Narrative to Alpha \hfill 9pp}
What does one exogenous signal add to a structurally narrow microstructure book? Three reasons it helps
without being individually strong: \textbf{timescale separation} (disjoint from the 0.005--0.1\,Hz
imbalance band), \textbf{effective breadth} ($\Neff=N/[1+(N-1)\rhobar]$ — a weak orthogonal signal beats
a strong redundant one), \textbf{partial evasion} of the adverse-selection barrier (patient execution
couples weakly to the fillable-state indicator). Same as-of join, same five gates, no privileges.
\caution{All three stated as hypotheses with FDR-corrected tests; no performance figures. Weak point:
$\rhobar$ must be estimated under \emph{stress}, per \#6.}

\opsec{6 \ process — The Process as an Analytical Unit \hfill 14pp}
Third first-class unit beside feature and algorithm: a typed, registry-bound contract certifying whether
something carries information about a precisely specified target. MI as currency (justified by
\textbf{Fano}, a hard lower bound on error). $\IR\approx\ICbar\sqrt{\Neff}$; \textbf{the binding
correlation is the stress-regime one, not the calm one}. Five mechanisms: CMI greedy selection,
interaction-information synergy mining, prefix residualization, Marchenko--Pastur-denoised PCs, capstone
decorrelated book reporting honest $\Neff$. Aspect$\times$horizon target taxonomy; derivative operators
under a program-level FDR ledger. \caution{First real surface: 284 BTC cells, 0 discoveries — BH at the
100-shuffle $p$-floor \emph{cannot} pass. Power problem, not a verdict.}

\opsec{7 \ lob\_predictive\_structure — Two Channels \hfill 8pp}
2.17\,M ticks/symbol. \textbf{Two empirically orthogonal channels}: directional ($\IC$ to $0.47$ at
1--5\,s, 8 independent axes, imbalance-led) and volatility ($\IC$ $0.35$, arrival intensity/activity/
toxicity) — each carries \emph{zero} of the other's information, on all three symbols. Spectral
localization to 0.005--0.1\,Hz, OU half-life 5--7\,s, cross-coherence 68\,s; \textbf{time-domain
aggregation destroys the signal}. Efficiency drift measured live (5\,s IC $.45\to.29$ as spreads tighten).
Execution boundary: maker-fill conditioning $\to0.03$, but \textbf{any-fill conditioning $\to0.52$} —
which isolates adverse selection rather than decay. \caution{OOS replication in progress, not done.}

\opsec{8 \ nat2\_exact\_map — Exact or Nothing \hfill 11pp}
Instrument for \#3. Two rules: \textbf{exact or nothing} (unobserved $\Rightarrow$ absent + coverage
deficit, never imputed) and \textbf{gates before models} (feed$\to$map$\to$magnet$\to$persistence$\to$
decay; the refusal is the product). WORM store, dual clocks $t^{\mathrm{event}}/t^{\mathrm{ingest}}$ so
lookahead is \emph{queryable}; hash-chained ledger (threat model is the researcher, not an attacker);
admissibility $=$ exists $\wedge$ passed $\wedge$ fresh. \code{gate feed} PASS 20/20; \textbf{\code{gate
map} FAIL} on \code{predictive} — an unevaluable criterion must not pass. Coverage .315/.380/.247;
derivation exact for 40.8\% of 2,926. Code at \code{~/nat2} (separate repo).
