\ophead{Exact or Nothing}
{Gate-Governed Measurement of the Liquidation Map on a Transparent Perpetual-Futures Venue}
{\code{nat2_exact_map_preprint.tex} $\cdot$ 11pp $\cdot$ Aug 2026 $\cdot$ code: \code{~/nat2} (separate repo)
 $\cdot$ numbers: \code{nat2/data/ledger.jsonl}}

\begin{multicols}{2}

\opsec{Thesis}
Cascade research is normally done on an \emph{estimated} liquidation map (aggregate OI $\times$ assumed
leverage mix). On Hyperliquid it need not be: per-position \code{liquidationPx} is published, mark comes
from a published oracle, any address's fills are public. Refuse estimation entirely and see what the
programme looks like — and what becomes visible that estimation hid.

\opsec{The two rules (enforced in code, not prose)}
\begin{opitems}
\item \textbf{Exact or nothing.} An unobserved position is \emph{absent} and enters the coverage deficit;
      it is never imputed. Asymmetry: an 80\%-right approximation gives a confidently wrong backtest; an
      acknowledged hole gives a coverage number, and a number can gate a decision.
\item \textbf{Gates before models.} \code{feed} $\to$ \code{map} $\to$ \code{magnet} $\to$
      \code{persistence} $\to$ \code{decay}. Each may end its branch; two may end the project. The
      refusal is the product.
\end{opitems}

\opsec{Substrate}
\kv{Two clocks.}{Every record carries $t^{\mathrm{event}}$ (venue) and $t^{\mathrm{ingest}}$ (first
knowable). Causal readable set $\mathcal{F}_T=\{r: t^{\mathrm{ingest}}(r)\le T\}$; a replay audit fails on
any diff. Lookahead becomes \emph{queryable}, not a code-review hope.}
\kv{WORM store.}{Hourly rotation, sha256 into a manifest on close, never reopened. Restart mid-hour
$\Rightarrow$ new part, so a changed digest always means corruption, never a bounced daemon. Per-stream
\code{seq} survives restarts $\Rightarrow$ a hole means records \emph{lost}.}
\kv{Ledger.}{$h_i=\mathrm{SHA256}(\mathrm{enc}(i,\tau_i,\kappa_i,\pi_i,h_{i-1}))$, $h_{-1}=0^{64}$.
Threat model is not an attacker — it is the researcher. The 14 variants tried before the one that worked
stay on the record.}
\kv{Admissibility.}{Run iff $V(g)$ exists $\wedge$ $\mathrm{pass}(g)=1$ $\wedge$ $t-\tau(g)\le\Delta$
($\Delta=24$h). Without the freshness clause a gate that passed once becomes a permanent licence.}

\opsec{Coverage, and the factor of two}
\[
  \widehat{\rho}_t=\frac{N_t}{s\cdot \mathrm{OI}_t},\qquad s\in\{1,2\}
\]
OI counts each contract once, but a wallet registry sees both sides $\Rightarrow$ outstanding notional is
$2\,\mathrm{OI}$. $s{=}2$ is conservative, is printed on every map, and is an \emph{open verify item}:
being wrong here is a factor of two on the one number the map is judged by.

\opsec{Measured (ledger, with dates)}
\begin{opfacts}
\code{gate feed}            & \textsf{PASS}, 20 checks, 0 failed \\
\code{gate map}             & \textsf{FAIL} on \code{predictive} \\
Coverage $s{=}2$            & BTC .315 / ETH .380 / SOL .247 (floor .25) \\
Derivation exact ($<10^{-4}$) & 40.8\% of 2,926 positions \\
\quad median / p90 rel err  & 0.152 / 0.921 \\
\quad no-isolated-margin    & 58\% of 1,074 cross positions \\
Registry                    & 3,596 wallets; sweep $\approx$5 min $=$ whole 1,200/min IP budget \\
Leaderboard probe 08-07     & 41,392 wallets; equity-2k $=$ 61\% OI / 28\% active; volume-2k $=$ 39\% / 46\%; union 69\% \\
\end{opfacts}
\caution{The 69\% probe is \emph{not} the coverage number. Its ratio to the ledger's 25--38\% is $\approx s$
itself — plausibly one-sided, unresolved. Report both, never the flattering one.}

\opsec{The finding (negative, and general)}
Of 48 observed liquidations the equity-seeded registry held
\[
  \mathbf{2.9\%}\ \text{of liquidated \emph{wallets}},\qquad
  \mathbf{21.9\%}\ \text{of liquidated \emph{notional}}
\]
($\$223{,}491$, 35 wallets; re-read next cycle 2.8\% / 21.9\% over 49). Mass and events are carried by
\emph{different populations}: the map wants whoever holds size, but whoever gets liquidated is small and
levered. So the OI rescaling of the heatmap paper cannot repair it — that correction assumes uniform
thinning, and this is biased in \emph{shape}. Generalises to any venue where positions are sampled rather
than enumerated: a single notional-denominated coverage scalar reports the flattering half.

\opsec{Why the headline is not believable yet}
Mapped notional fraction reads $0.0\%$ — and that is \emph{confounded, not informative}: a liquidated
wallet no longer holds the position, so a snapshot taken after the event has nothing to have predicted.
Cannot yet separate ``different populations'' from ``we looked too late''.

\opsec{The discriminating experiment (pre-stated in the ledger)}
\code{nat2 cycle --snapshot-every 6h --scan-every 1h}, one overlap reading appended per pass; needs
$\sim$a day.
\begin{opnums}
\item mapped frac climbs off 0 $\Rightarrow$ per-position scoring works, instrument stands.
\item mapped pinned at 0, notional holds $\approx$22\% $\Rightarrow$ we see the right wallets but never
      \emph{before} they blow up $\Rightarrow$ demote to cluster-level scoring (weaker, still falsifiable).
\item notional frac collapses too $\Rightarrow$ seed a third registry by \emph{liquidation risk}
      (margin ratio / distance-to-liq), computable from data already held.
\end{opnums}
Only (1) validates as specified. The discriminating variable is calendar time under capture — hence
capture runs while the rest is built; a point-in-time series cannot be recovered later.

\opsec{Where liquidations are observable}
Public tape carries no marker. \code{userFills} does, twice: the liquidated wallet's own \code{dir}
string, and — far more abundantly — the \emph{counterparty's} fill, a \code{liquidation} object naming
\code{liquidatedUser}, \code{markPx}, method. Measured 08-08 over 117,485 fills / 70 wallets: exactly
\emph{one} self-side \code{dir}, but 16/70 wallets had taken the other side. Efficient observer set is a
handful of high-volume MMs, not the registry. REST has no per-user cap (WS caps at 15).
Scoring drops events predating their snapshot and reports the count: 24 of the first 48.

\opsec{Venue-native quantities with no CEX analogue}
$\pi_t=(p^{\mathrm{mark}}-p^{\mathrm{oracle}})/p^{\mathrm{oracle}}$ replaces a second-venue sidecar (no
second clock/cost model) $\cdot$ hourly funding is first-order at cascade horizons $\cdot$
$\Delta^{\mathrm{HLP}}$ \emph{measures} unabsorbed cascade flow (exhaustion observed, not inferred)
$\cdot$ one address's perp+spot+vault visible $\Rightarrow$ intra-venue hedges detectable.

\opsec{Open / unverified (treated as live defects)}
$s$ convention $\cdot$ liq-formula residual (size-tiered maintenance margin prime suspect) $\cdot$ mark
construction \& which price triggers $\cdot$ funding clamp/cadence $\cdot$ rate-limit table (stamped,
warns at 90d) $\cdot$ WS connections per IP $\cdot$ node-data cost (sizes M3).
Gaps: \code{l2book} 45 records/70s across 3 coins (sparser than hinted); clock skew flipped
$-196$ms$\to+395$ms, within 2s tolerance but unexplained.

\opsec{Layers (calls go downward only)}
\code{L-1} capture (WORM, dual-stamped, daemon) $\cdot$ \code{L0} features (pure bar$\to$vector, causality
contract) $\cdot$ \code{L1} experts (calibrated $p$ or score) $\cdot$ \code{L2} meta (Hedge / fixed-share
over net-of-cost expert PnL) $\cdot$ \code{L3} allocate (vol target $\to$ capacity cap $\to$
\code{maxLeverage} cap $\to$ liq-distance floor) $\cdot$ \code{L4} execute (simulator $|$ testnet $|$
mainnet, flag-gated off) $\cdot$ \code{L5} ledger. Downward-only is what lets simulator and live path share
code honestly.

\opsec{Milestones — each ends at a gate}
\code{M0} client + capture + WORM $\to$ \code{gate feed} \textbf{done} $\cdot$ \code{M1} reconstruction +
map + coverage $\to$ \code{gate map} \textbf{built, FAIL} $\cdot$ \code{M2} labels + magnet experts +
purged WFO $\to$ \code{gate magnet} $\cdot$ \code{M3} node backfill + vault tagging $\to$
\code{gate persistence} (\emph{go/no-go for smart money}) $\cdot$ \code{M4} cohort flow + decay study $\to$
\code{gate decay} $\cdot$ \code{M5} meta + sizer + simulator + testnet $\cdot$ \code{M6} TUI.
M3/M4 precede M5 deliberately: each is $\sim$a week and each may end its branch.

\opsec{Expert contract}
\code{fit} / \code{predict} / \textbf{\code{baseline()} mandatory} — GBDT-vs-$\sgn(\mathrm{imb})$ and
clone-vs-raw-flow are the same rule, written once, enforced for every expert by \code{nat2 eval}. Must
clear $\text{edge} > 2(\text{fees}+\text{funding}+\text{slippage})/\sigma$ net, or it does not enter the
pool. LightGBM depth 3 (short history punishes variance; value is in features and labels). Purged
walk-forward, embargo $h$, isotonic calibration on OOS folds only. Two-barrier race (Stage A), triple
barrier on touch (Stage B), uniqueness weights — cascade episodes overlap heavily. Barriers evaluate on
\emph{mark}, because that is what the position experiences.

\opsec{Named risks}
Reconstruction drift (continuous sampled reconcile; drift \textsf{FAIL}s \code{gate feed}) $\cdot$
cross/isolated modelled wrong (both diffed vs HL's own \code{liquidationPx}) $\cdot$ overlapping-cascade
overfit $\cdot$ \textbf{crowding — the exact map is exact for hunters too} (edge is the conditional odds
\emph{after} the touch; fixed-share demotes decayed experts; our own liq distance is watched by the same
code that builds the map) $\cdot$ oracle-manipulation tail, JELLY-style $\cdot$ short history (wider
embargo, smaller models, \emph{never} pad with CEX data) $\cdot$ single-venue operational risk, accepted
explicitly.

supply the adverse-selection barrier the execution layer is built around $\cdot$ \#6 process is the same
gate discipline one level up.
