\opsec{Thesis}
The gap between statistical predictability and economic exploitability, measured rather than asserted.
236 engineered features, 100\,ms cadence, BTC/ETH/SOL perps, horizons 1\,s--15\,min. \textbf{The central
result is negative}, and it is the reason the whole programme pivoted to execution.

\opsec{The headline pair}
\[
  \IC_{\text{uncond}} \approx 0.45
  \;\;\longrightarrow\;\;
  \IC_{\text{cond}} \approx 0.03
\]
\vspace{-4pt}
\begin{center}
{\scriptsize condition on states where a directionally-correct passive (mid-cross) fill occurs}
\end{center}
Holds on all three symbols. This is a \emph{structural adverse-selection barrier}, not an estimation
artifact: the states in which you get filled are systematically the states in which you were wrong.
\textbf{Execution, not signal quality, is the binding constraint.}

\opsec{Signal structure}
\begin{opitems}
\item Order-book imbalance dominates directionally; $\IC\approx0.45$ at 1--5\,s, decaying to $\approx0$
      by 5\,min.
\item Same feature hierarchy across BTC/ETH/SOL — universality, not a per-symbol fit.
\item Clean directional/volatility orthogonality (developed fully in \#7).
\item Robustness: 7 of 8 checks pass for BTC, 6 of 8 for ETH and SOL.
\end{opitems}

\opsec{The five-gate replication protocol}
Every candidate passes all five or lands in a documented graveyard \emph{with its rejection reason}.
\begin{opitems}
\item[\textbf{G1}] \textbf{Discovery} — rank IC over an adaptive threshold \emph{and} incremental
      $\mathrm{dIC}\ge0.05$ beyond already-registered signals.
\item[\textbf{G2}] \textbf{Cost} — gross return/trade $\ge 0.1$\,bps. Kills statistically real signals
      too small to pay for themselves.
\item[\textbf{G3}] \textbf{Temporal} — IC holds on $\ge1$ further date (White-2000 reality check in
      spirit).
\item[\textbf{G4}] \textbf{Cross-symbol} — IC holds on $\ge1$ other symbol.
\item[\textbf{G5}] \textbf{Correlation dedup} — $\rho<0.7$ against every registered signal. Enforces
      breadth, not just strength.
\end{opitems}

\opsec{Multiple-testing machinery}
BH-FDR at $q=0.05$ across all tests each cycle: order $p_{(1)}\le\dots\le p_{(m)}$, take
$j^*=\max\{j:p_{(j)}\le jq/m\}$. Threshold \emph{adapts} as the registry strengthens:
\[
  \mathrm{minIC}(t)=\max\bigl(\text{floor},\ \operatorname{median}\{\IC_i: i\in R(t)\}\times0.8\bigr)
\]
plus continuous decay monitoring on registered signals. The apparatus exists to suppress the
multiple-testing bias that pervades empirical asset pricing — a discovery platform that tests
thousands of hypotheses \emph{needs} this or it manufactures winners.
