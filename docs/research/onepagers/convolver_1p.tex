\ophead{Event-Aligned Singular Value Decomposition}
{for Data-Driven Pattern Kernel Discovery in Cryptocurrency Perpetual Futures}
{\code{convolver_preprint.tex} $\cdot$ 15pp $\cdot$ May 2026 $\cdot$ the P1 camera-ready target $\cdot$
 lead preprint for Teichmann / Bölcskei / Malamud / Cheridito / Zdeborová / Krzakala / Filipović}

\begin{multicols}{2}

\opsec{Thesis}
Discover predictive multi-candle price patterns without hand-designing them. Define events
\emph{analytically} (so they stay interpretable), extract aligned OHLCV windows, decompose into four
signal channels, let SVD find the dominant \emph{shapes}, then gate on forward-return correlation with
FDR control. Inherits the interpretability of classical TA and the statistical efficiency of learned
bases.

\opsec{Pipeline}
\begin{opnums}
\item \textbf{Six analytical event types} — breakout bull/bear, turtle-soup false breakout bull/bear,
      trap bull/bear. Analytically defined, not learned $\Rightarrow$ interpretable.
\item \textbf{Four channels} per window: body, upper wick, lower wick, volume.
\item \textbf{Event-aligned matrix} $\to$ \textbf{SVD} $\to$ dominant shape variations.
\item \textbf{IC gate} with Benjamini--Hochberg FDR ($q=0.05$) on forward returns.
\item \textbf{Online scoring} by cosine similarity against the surviving kernels.
\end{opnums}

\opsec{The structural safeguard (the argument to a supervisor)}
\textbf{SVD operates on price geometry alone — it never sees forward returns.} Basis discovery and
label-fitting are separated by construction, so the usual ``the shape was fit to the outcome'' objection
does not apply. Returns enter only at the gating step, where FDR is applied. That separation is the
methodological point of the paper.

\opsec{Data}
\begin{opfacts}
Asset / venue & BTC-USD perp, Hyperliquid \\
Ticks & 7,235,623 at 100\,ms \\
Candles & 12,059 at 60\,s \\
Params & $W{=}20$, $N{=}20$, $K{=}3$, $\alpha{=}1.0$, $N_{\mathrm{atr}}{=}14$ \\
Events & 1,775 total (147.2 per 1000 candles) \\
\end{opfacts}
Breakouts most frequent (bear 522 $>$ bull 418); traps rarest (136/172) — consistent with stricter
multi-bar confirmation. All types clear $n_{\min}=100$ for stable SVD.

\opsec{Result: 6 of 158 candidates survive FDR}
\begin{opfacts}
Turtle bull, body, $k{=}0$ & $\IC=+0.203$ \\
Turtle bull, body, $k{=}6$ & $\IC=-0.201$ \\
Turtle bear, lower wick, $k{=}0$ & $\IC=-0.262$ \\
Trap bull, upper wick, $k{=}6$ & $\IC=+0.357$ \\
Trap bull, volume, $k{=}0$ & $\IC=-0.270$ \\
Trap bear, body, $k{=}7$ & $\IC=-0.272$ \\
\end{opfacts}
\textbf{No breakout kernel survives} — despite high explained variance. The most-traded pattern in
retail TA carries no forward-return information after multiple-testing correction. Turtle-soup and traps
dominate; $|\IC|\in[0.20,0.36]$. All 6 have max cosine similarity $<0.5$ against each other (genuinely
distinct shapes, not one kernel six ways).

\opsec{Rolling-SVD stability}
$\bar\rho$ over rolling windows: trap bull $0.941$, turtle bull $0.920$, breakout bear $0.855$ (stable);
turtle bear $0.719$ (borderline); breakout bull $0.692$, trap bear $0.669$ (unstable). \textbf{The event
types with the strongest IC are also the most temporally stable} — encouraging, and not something the
method was tuned to produce.

\opsec{The caveat that matters most}
\caution{\textbf{No kernel meets the robustness threshold} on the IS/OOS decay test. OOS ICs collapse:
breakout bull $+0.243\to-0.010$; trap bull upper wick $-0.474\to+0.126$ (\emph{sign flip}); trap bull
volume $-0.449\to+0.145$ (sign flip). Best decay ratios are turtle-type at $0.26$--$0.38$, still under
threshold. So: 6 kernels are \emph{statistically significant in-sample after FDR}, and \emph{none} is
demonstrated to generalise. Do not let the abstract's ``6 significant kernels'' be read as 6 usable
signals — the paper is explicit; a skimming reader will not be.}

\opsec{Validation framework}
Walk-forward: \textbf{discovery} on $t\le T_{\text{train}}$, \textbf{validation} strictly on
$t>T_{\text{train}}$ — the split is what produced the negative robustness result above rather than
hiding it. \textbf{Rolling-SVD stability} re-estimates the basis on moving windows and correlates
successive bases ($\bar\rho$), which tests whether a ``shape'' is a real recurring geometry or a
window-specific artifact. A kernel can be significant and unstable; both are reported.

\opsec{Analytical basis dictionary}
Discovered kernels are matched against closed-form families — polynomial, cubic, exponential
($a\in[3,8]$) — plus an alignment diagnostic. Purpose: say \emph{what shape was found}, in words, rather
than shipping an opaque 20-dim vector. This is the interpretability half of the pitch and the part a
classical-TA reader can check.

\opsec{Why it suits a maths/signal-processing supervisor}
Matched filtering + event-aligned averaging is the FINDCHIRP / gravitational-wave template-bank idea
transplanted to price geometry; the basis is learned by SVD rather than derived from a physical model,
and the multiple-testing control is what makes the transplant honest. That framing is the pitch for
Bölcskei (signal-processing theory) and Teichmann (ML in finance).
