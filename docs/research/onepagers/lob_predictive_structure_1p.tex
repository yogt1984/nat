\ophead{Predictive Structure of the Perpetual-Futures Limit Order Book}
{Universality, Orthogonality, and the Execution Boundary}
{\code{lob_predictive_structure_preprint.tex} $\cdot$ 8pp $\cdot$ Jul 2026 $\cdot$ measurement-first
 sharpening of \#2 $\cdot$ the only paper in the set that cites inline (natbib)}

\begin{multicols}{2}

\opsec{Thesis}
A 236-feature census of the book at 100\,ms cadence (2.17\,M ticks/symbol, BTC/ETH/SOL), horizons
1\,s--100\,min. The book carries \textbf{two empirically orthogonal predictive channels}, and their
monetization is sharply bounded at the point of execution.

\opsec{The factorization result (the headline)}
\begin{opfacts}
\textbf{Directional channel} & rank $\IC$ up to $\mathbf{0.47}$ at 1--5\,s \\
\quad carried by & imbalance family, 8 independent axes \\
\quad volatility info & \textbf{zero} \\[2pt]
\textbf{Volatility channel} & peak $\IC$ $\mathbf{0.35}$ at 5\,s \\
\quad carried by & arrival intensity, activity counts, toxicity \\
\quad directional info & \textbf{zero} \\
\end{opfacts}
Disjoint feature families, on all three symbols. \textbf{Sign and magnitude of short-horizon returns are
independently predictable from disjoint feature sets} — which is what licenses a two-block
(sign $\times$ size) forecasting architecture instead of one joint model.

\opsec{Spectral localization}
The entire directional IC lives in the $0.005$--$0.1$\,Hz band. OU half-life $5$--$7$\,s. Dominant
cross-coherence at $68$\,s. \textbf{Time-domain aggregation destroys the signal} — if you bar it up, you
have thrown the alpha away before you started.

\opsec{Efficiency drift, measured in real time}
Half-life \emph{shortens} May$\to$June as spreads tighten in a maturing venue: 5\,s IC $0.45\to0.29$ while
1\,s holds or improves. Not a stationary edge — a decaying one, and the decay is observable while it
happens. Anything built on the 5\,s horizon has a shelf life.

\opsec{The execution boundary}
\[
  \IC \;=\; 0.45
  \;\;\xrightarrow{\text{cond.\ on directionally-consistent \emph{maker} fill}}\;\; 0.03
\]
\[
  \IC \;=\; 0.45
  \;\;\xrightarrow{\text{cond.\ on the presence of \emph{any} fill}}\;\; \mathbf{0.52}
\]
The second line is the one that matters and is unique to this paper. Any-fill conditioning \emph{raises}
IC; directional-fill conditioning collapses it. That pair isolates \textbf{adverse selection} — not signal
decay, not estimation noise — as the binding constraint. Regime gating on \code{ent_book_shape} partially
reopens it ($+22\%$).

\opsec{Novelty ledger (write related-work from this)}
Known, must cite, do not re-claim: imbalance/OFI predicts short-horizon returns (Cont--Kukanov--Stoikov
2014; deep-LOB literature). New here:
\begin{opnums}
\item Orthogonality factorization across 3 symbols.
\item Universality at scale — 207-feature census, 6 horizons, 3 symbols, bootstrap CIs, compressing to
      8 independent directional axes.
\item Spectral localization + OU half-life.
\item Efficiency drift measured in real time.
\item The execution boundary as a \emph{mechanism} section, not the headline.
\end{opnums}

\opsec{Implications the paper states}
Two-block (sign $\times$ size) forecasting $\cdot$ entropy-gated conditioning $\cdot$ execution-timing
overlays for \emph{slower} strategies — i.e.\ use the fast signal to time entries for something that
isn't fast, since the fast thing itself cannot be filled profitably.

\opsec{Measurement platform}
Rust ingestion engine on the full book + trade stream, \textbf{236-dim feature vector per symbol every
100\,ms}, 21 categories: book imbalance at multiple depths, raw depth, microstructure dynamics (queue
position, imbalance velocity), trade flow / aggressor ratios, VWAP deviation, entropy (permutation,
book-shape), Hawkes-type arrival intensity, realized \& range volatility, spread and toxicity proxies,
funding/OI state, cross-symbol aggregates. \textbf{End-to-end feature latency $<80$\,ms at p99.}

\opsec{Census window}
Three contiguous days, 2026-05-19 to 05-21. 2.17\,M ticks/symbol at 100\,ms. Spearman rank IC vs future
mid returns at six horizons (1\,s, 5\,s, 30\,s, 1\,m, 5\,m, 15\,m; selected features also 100\,m) on
$\sim$21,708 subsampled points/symbol — \textbf{one per $\sim$10\,s, deliberately, to control overlap}.
Overlapping samples are how this kind of census inflates its own significance; the subsampling is the
defence.

\opsec{Robustness detail}
\begin{opfacts}
Per-day rolling IC @5\,s & BTC $+0.433\pm0.061$ \\
 & ETH $+0.400\pm0.098$ \\
 & SOL $+0.355\pm0.071$ \\
Bootstrap 95\% CI (BTC, \code{imbalance_qty_l1}) & $[0.442,\,0.465]$, 1,000 resamples \\
Time of day & no dead zones; weakest 12--16 UTC, strongest 04--08 UTC \\
Vol regime (median split) & low $+.458/+.435/+.391$ vs high $+.422/+.394/+.342$ \\
\end{opfacts}
Feature rank ordering preserved across symbols and subwindows — this is what ``universal'' means
operationally.

\opsec{The one systematic failure}
\caution{Validation matrix: BTC 7/8, ETH 6/8, SOL 6/8. The failing cell is the \emph{same} one every
time — \textbf{temporal out-of-sample drift}: IC delta $-0.17$ across a six-week gap against a $0.10$
tolerance. Partially attributable to the efficiency drift documented above, i.e.\ the failure and one of
the findings may be the same phenomenon. Do not report 7/8 without naming which cell fails.}
