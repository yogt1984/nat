1. IDEA
-------
Mean-reversion band channel at strategy timescale: midline = rolling VWAP (or MA)
of mid over W minutes; band scale sigma = rolling std of the deviation
(mid - midline)/midline; rest a limit BUY when deviation <= -k*sigma and a limit
SELL when deviation >= +k*sigma; exit at midline (or opposite band, or +k_exit).
This is the classical "regression channel / VWAP bands" idea, rebuilt under NAT
discipline: the band multiple k is a SWEPT parameter judged by imported gates,
never a number copied from folklore.

2. WHY THE GENERIC "2 SD" ADVICE DOES NOT TRANSFER UNMODIFIED
-------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Fees. On Hyperliquid (config/costs.toml, via load_costs() ONLY):
    maker = 0.2 bps REBATE per side -> a maker round trip EARNS ~0.4 bps.
    The generic "cover 5-10 bps maker fees" break-even framing is void here.
    The binding cost is ADVERSE SELECTION (FINDINGS.md §2: directional-fill
    conditioning collapses IC 0.45 -> ~0.03), plus failure-to-revert tail risk.
(b) Percent-scale folklore. "2 SD = 2.5-4% move" assumes 4H/daily channels.
    At NAT's 60-min channel the measured sigma is ~15-19 bps (see §3), so band
    distances are tens of bps, not percent. All arithmetic must be in bps.
(c) The flow_vwap_deviation FEATURE is a 5-second VWAP (rust flow.rs) — it is
    NOT this unit's channel. The channel is constructed at strategy timescale
    from bars; the 5 s feature stays what it is (census axis #5).

---------------------------------------------------

- Exit: midline exit vs +/-k_exit sweep; time-stop at W; hard stop at channel
  break (k_stop) — all swept.
