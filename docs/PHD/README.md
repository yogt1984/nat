## The two games

These are not one list. Admission works differently on each side, and the sequencing follows the
mechanism, not the quality of fit.

| | EPFL / SFI | ETH / UZH |
|---|---|---|
| **How you get in** | **EDFI**, a structured doctoral program. A committee admits you; the advisor is settled later. | The **professor hires and funds you** directly. |
| **Deadline** | **Jan 15 2027** (backstop Mar 31) | None — contact *is* the application |
| **Implication** | Apply on Jan 15 **whether or not anyone replies.** A silent inbox is not a rejection. | A reply is the whole game. Nothing happens without one. |
